# How to film your main video (so the Shorts edit great)

Read this once, then record. Total time: 20 minutes.

## Set up (2 minutes)

1. **Light**: sit facing a window, in daylight. The window is IN FRONT of you, not behind. No lamp needed. Never sit with the window behind your head.
2. **Phone**: back or front camera, at eye level (stack books). Landscape (sideways) is best for the long video; head and shoulders fill about two thirds of the frame, eyes in the upper third.
3. **Quiet**: close the door, turn off the fan, phone on Do Not Disturb.
4. **Sound**: 30-40 cm from your mouth is fine. No music playing.
5. Clean the lens with your shirt. Seriously.

## How to speak

- Short sentences. One idea per line. Max about 12 words.
- **Pause for one second between lines** and keep still. The editor cuts at the pauses.
- Smile on the first line, then talk to ONE friend, not to a camera.
- Slightly more energy than normal: it looks flat on video otherwise.
- Say numbers out loud ("20 scenes", "14 days", "21 screen sizes"): numbers become the on-screen titles.
- Made a mistake? Do NOT stop. Pause, then say that line again cleanly. The transcript lets the edit pick the good one.
- Do 2 to 3 full takes. Keep them all.

## What to say (fill in the blanks, keep each line short)

1. **Hook** (first 5 seconds): "This is [my life / my product] as a [surprising thing]." / "I built [thing] in [number] days."
2. **What it is**: one line, then one line on how it works.
3. **The fun detail**: something hidden, weird or personal (cats, easter eggs, a hobby). People remember this.
4. **The failure story** (very important): "My first attempt was [wrong thing]. It failed because [reason]. So I [fix]." Failure stories get comments.
5. **The hard part**: the unglamorous thing that took longest, with a number.
6. **The result**: where people can see it.
7. **Call to action**: "The link is in the description." then a question for comments ("Which scene would you pick?").

Aim for 90 to 150 seconds of talking. Shorts are cut from it: you need at least three different mini-stories (what it is, what went wrong, the hard part).

## After recording

- Send the file as-is (do not trim, filter or compress). Share via cable, Drive or WhatsApp "document", not as a normal photo/video message (it gets compressed).
- Write down anything you improvised or added; the transcript will catch it, but tell your editor.
- Tell your editor the three names/words that are easy to misspell (your name, product names).

## If you also need screen b-roll

Do not film your screen with the phone. Ask your editor (or Claude) to record the website/app in a browser at 1920x1080, smooth scroll, 60 seconds. Your face video stays the face video.
