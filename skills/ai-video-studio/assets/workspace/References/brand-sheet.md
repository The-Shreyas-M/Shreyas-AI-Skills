# Brand sheet (edit me; Claude reads this before every edit)

Fill this once from videos you like (ask Claude: "study the videos in References and update this sheet": it detects cuts with ffmpeg, samples frames, transcribes, and reports numbers). Use free fonts and your own colours.

- Accent colour: #FFD400 (yellow). Text: white with black outline.
- Caption font: Impact (fallback Montserrat ExtraBold, bundled). 1-3 words at a time, centred around 2/3 down, active word in accent colour.
- Hook: first 3 seconds = the strongest line + a 2-line title in accent colour.
- Cuts: remove false starts and dead air; pauses over 0.25 s trimmed to about 0.1 s in long videos.
- Speed: Shorts 1.1x. Zooms: open snap, then push/punch every 3-5 s.
- Music: calm phonk for Shorts only, ducked under the voice, -14 LUFS final. None on long videos.
- Safe zones: nothing important in the top 220 px, bottom 420 px, right 120 px.
- Never alter the owner's face (no darkening or colour grade on the face).
