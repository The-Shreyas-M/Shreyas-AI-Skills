AI VIDEO EDITOR: SKILL CHEAT SHEET
==================================
Remember only ONE thing: type  /ai-video  in Claude Code. It asks what you have and what you want, then uses the right tool below.

SKILLS (in ~/.claude/skills)
  ai-video                 The single entry point. Routes any video job to the right tool and keeps token use low.
  ai-video-studio          The workflow + workspace folder: drop videos, subagent edits, you approve, posting queue.
  shorts-from-main-video   Your own talking-head video -> Shorts/Reels (b-roll on top, face below, captions, titles, zooms, music).
  clip-video               Long video or YouTube link -> best moments as Shorts; AI picks moments, face-tracked reframing.
  youtube                  Titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, SEO, content strategy (third-party).

MODULES (in ~/.claude/tools, read on demand by /ai-video)
  claude-animation-skills  No footage needed: stick-figure explainer, 3D cartoon explainer, graph, map route, exploded view, minimal line art.
  brag-slim                Turns a project directory or website URL into a short launch video with music and share copy.
  HyperFrames              The HTML-to-MP4 renderer under the animation tools (npx hyperframes).

WORKSPACE (~/AI Video Editor)
  Raw Footage/             your originals (never modified)
  Exports/<project>/       finished videos + .srt + post info
  References/brand-sheet.md  your style rules (edit once)
  posting/                 post.py: schedule/post to YouTube (official API), Instagram (optional); accounts.json, schedule.csv
  CLAUDE.md                the rules every Claude chat follows in this folder

COMMON PROMPTS
  /ai-video
  "Make 3 Shorts from Raw Footage/talk.mp4"
  "Clip this YouTube video: <link>"
  "Make a faceless 30-second explainer about <topic>, here is my voiceover"
  "Make a launch video for my website <url>"
  "Schedule these 40 clips on <account>, 2 per day from Monday"
  Then: watch Exports/, say "approved", answer "which accounts / when?".
